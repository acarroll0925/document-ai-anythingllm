# What It Can and Can't Answer — Juniper Table Demo Workspace

*One page. This sheet ships with every client delivery, filled in for
their documents. This copy is filled in for the demo set.*

## It CAN answer — from these documents, with citations

| Topic | Source document |
|---|---|
| Scheduling rules, shift swaps, call-outs | Employee Handbook §2 |
| PTO amounts, request lead time, blackout dates | Employee Handbook §3 |
| Uniform and conduct rules | Employee Handbook §4 |
| Food holding temperatures, handwashing | Food Safety & Allergen Policy §1–2 |
| The exact nut-allergy procedure (and other allergens) | Food Safety & Allergen Policy §4–5 |
| Event-day call times and timeline | Event Day Run-of-Show Guide §1–2 |
| Delivery radius and the mileage fee | Delivery & Vehicle Policy §2 |
| Deposit amount and due date | Client Booking Terms §1 |
| Cancellation refunds at any notice period | Client Booking Terms §3 |
| Menu contents and exact per-guest prices | Menu & Pricing Sheet |
| Incident form name and filing deadline | Incident Report Procedure §2 |
| New-hire steps (food handler card, shadowing) | New Hire Onboarding Checklist |

## It CAN'T answer — and will say so, plainly

- Anything not in the documents: salaries or the owner's pay, recipes,
  ingredient lists beyond what a document states, tax or legal questions.
- Anything about a real business, a real client, or a real event — the
  demo company is fictional, and a client workspace only knows the
  documents the client puts in it.
- Anything time-sensitive it wasn't given: today's schedule, current
  bookings, weather, prices from a newer menu than the one uploaded.
- Judgement calls: whether an exception *should* be made. It quotes the
  policy; a person decides the exception.

## The rule that makes it trustworthy

If the answer isn't in the documents, the answer is **"I don't have that
in the provided documents."** — never a guess wearing a citation's
clothes. The test set in `test-set.md` includes two questions designed
to prove this, and one question designed to trap it into agreeing with
a false premise. It has to pass all three before this demo is shown to
anyone.
