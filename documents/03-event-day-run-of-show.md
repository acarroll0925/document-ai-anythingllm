# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Event Day Run-of-Show Guide

*The standard clock for a full-service event. The Event Captain adjusts times for drop-off and partial-service events, but the order of operations never changes.*

## 1. Call Times

- For full-service events, **crew call is 3 hours before guest arrival**.
- **The Event Captain arrives 30 minutes before the crew** to open the venue, walk the floor plan, and start the temperature log.
- Everyone signs in with the Captain on arrival. If you are running more than 10 minutes late, phone the Captain — do not text.

## 2. Sample Timeline (5:00 PM Guest Arrival)

| Time | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1:30 PM | Event Captain arrives; venue walk-through, floor plan check |
| 2:00 PM | Crew call; unload and set stations |
| 3:00 PM | Kitchen line set; cold food staged in coolers |
| 4:00 PM | Hot food fired; buffet or plating line dressed |
| 4:30 PM | Final temperature log; staff meal in the kitchen |
| 4:45 PM | Doors check — Captain confirms headcount setup against the booking sheet |
| 5:00 PM | Guest arrival; service begins |

## 3. Service Sequence

1. Beverages and the first station open as guests arrive.
2. The Captain watches the room and calls refills before a tray is empty — never after a guest has asked twice.
3. Allergy plates are handled only under the Food Safety & Allergen Policy (red pick, Captain carries).
4. The Captain — not the client, not a guest — decides when the buffet closes, based on the contracted service window.

## 4. Breakdown and Load-Out

- Breakdown begins when the last guest departs or the contracted service window ends, whichever comes first.
- Target: **load-out complete within 45 minutes** of breakdown start.
- Client's rentals, décor, and leftover food the client has claimed are packed separately and labeled before our equipment is loaded.
- The Captain does a final walk-through with lights on and photographs the empty room before locking up. Nothing leaves the checklist unchecked because the night ran long.
