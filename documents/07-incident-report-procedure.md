# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Incident Report Procedure

*An incident is any injury, illness, property damage, vehicle accident, or safety complaint at an event, at the kitchen, or in transit — no matter how minor it seems at the time.*

## 1. Immediate Steps

1. **Care first.** Attend to anyone hurt. Call 911 for anything beyond basic first aid — never wait to see if it gets better.
2. **Notify the Event Captain immediately.** If the Captain is the one involved, notify the Operations Manager directly.
3. **Make the scene safe** (clear the spill, move the crowd, stop the equipment) and, if it is safe to do so, photograph the scene before cleanup.

## 2. The Report — Form IR-1

- Every incident is documented on **Form IR-1**, our standard incident report form, kept in the event binder and the van glove box.
- **The Event Captain completes Form IR-1 and submits it to the Operations Manager before the end of the same shift.** A report written from memory the next day is a late report.
- The form records: what happened, who was involved, any witnesses, first aid given, and photographs taken. Facts only — no guesses about fault.

## 3. Follow-Up

- The Operations Manager reviews every Form IR-1 **within 2 business days** and contacts anyone involved who needs follow-up.
- If an incident involves a guest's allergy or a food-safety concern, the event allergen log and temperature logs for that event are attached to the report.
- Staff are never disciplined for reporting an incident promptly. Late or missing reports are a conduct issue under the Employee Handbook.
