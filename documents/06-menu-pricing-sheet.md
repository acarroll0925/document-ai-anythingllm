# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Menu & Pricing Sheet

*Current as of January 2026. Prices are per guest unless stated otherwise, before the service fee and tax. Staff may quote these prices exactly as written.*

## Full-Service Menus

*Full-service menus carry a **25-guest minimum** and include setup, service staff, and breakdown.*

### Southern Harvest Supper — $34.00 per guest

- Herb-roasted chicken with pan gravy
- Slow-smoked pulled pork with two house sauces
- Little Gem salad with Juniper House Vinaigrette
- Stone-ground grits, green beans with bacon, skillet cornbread

### Garden Brunch — $26.50 per guest

- Buttermilk biscuit bar with sausage gravy and fruit preserves
- Vegetable frittata and breakfast potatoes
- Fresh fruit platter and yogurt with granola
- Orange juice and lemonade included

### Smokehouse Feast — $41.00 per guest

- Brisket and baby back ribs from the smoker
- Baked beans, collard greens, mac and cheese
- Chopped slaw and pickles, yeast rolls
- Banana pudding for dessert

## Stations and Add-Ons

- **Sweet Tea & Lemonade Station — $4.75 per guest**
- **Coffee & Hot Tea Service — $3.25 per guest**
- **Late-Night Biscuit Bar — $8.00 per guest** (available for events ending after 9 PM)

## Service Fee and Notes

- A **20% service fee** applies to all full-service events. The service fee is not a gratuity.
- Drop-off service (no staff on site) is available for the Southern Harvest Supper and Garden Brunch menus at the same per-guest prices, with a $75 delivery-and-setup charge inside the standard service radius.
- Seasonal menus rotate twice a year; prices on a signed booking agreement are honored for that event even if this sheet changes afterward.
