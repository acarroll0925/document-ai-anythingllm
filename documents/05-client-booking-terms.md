# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Client Booking Terms

*These terms are part of every signed booking agreement. Staff may quote them to clients exactly as written here.*

## 1. Holding a Date and the Deposit

- A date is held only when the signed booking agreement and the deposit are both received. Verbal holds expire after 48 hours.
- **The deposit is 25% of the estimated event total, due within 7 days of signing** the booking agreement.
- The estimated total is based on the client's expected guest count and chosen menu at booking; it is adjusted to the final count under Section 2.

## 2. Final Numbers and Final Payment

- The **final guest count is due 10 days before the event**. We prepare for the final count plus 5%.
- The **final balance is due 7 days before the event**. If the final balance is not received, we may treat the booking as cancelled by the client under Section 3.
- Guest counts may increase after the deadline if the kitchen confirms capacity; counts may not decrease after the final-count deadline.

## 3. Cancellation and Refunds

If the client cancels, the deposit refund depends on how far before the event date the cancellation is received in writing:

- **More than 30 days before the event: full refund of the deposit.**
- **14 to 30 days before the event: 50% refund of the deposit.**
- **Fewer than 14 days before the event: no refund of the deposit.**

Amounts paid beyond the deposit are refunded in full at every tier. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days of the written cancellation.

## 4. Date Changes

- A client may move their event **once**, with at least 30 days' notice, and the full deposit transfers to the new date.
- Date changes with fewer than 30 days' notice are treated as a cancellation under Section 3, and a new deposit applies to the new date.

## 5. What the Client Provides

- Venue access at the agreed load-in time (see the Run-of-Show Guide), a water source, and one 20-amp circuit for hot-holding equipment.
- One contact person on site who can make decisions during the event.
