# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Employee Handbook

*Juniper Table Catering Co., Dahlonega, Georgia. Effective January 2026. This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time staff.*

## 1. Welcome

Juniper Table Catering Co. was founded in 2016 by our owner, Marla Quinn, with one rule that still runs the company: feed people like they're family, and run the event like it's our own. We cater weddings, reunions, corporate lunches, and celebrations across North Georgia from our kitchen at 412 Foundry Lane in Dahlonega.

Questions about anything in this handbook go to the Operations Manager, Dana Whitfield, first. Dana's answer is the company's answer.

## 2. Scheduling and Shifts

- Event schedules are posted **two weeks ahead** in the scheduling app every other Friday.
- If you need to swap a shift, find your own replacement and get the swap approved by the Operations Manager at least 48 hours before the event. An unapproved swap is treated as a no-show.
- Call-outs for illness must be phoned in — not texted — at least 4 hours before your call time.
- Two no-shows in a rolling 90-day period is grounds for removal from the schedule.

## 3. Time Off

- After **12 months of continuous service**, full-time employees receive **10 paid time-off (PTO) days** per year. Part-time employees receive PTO on a prorated basis after the same 12 months.
- **PTO requests must be submitted in writing at least 14 days in advance.** Requests are approved in the order received; no more than two staff may be off for the same event weekend.
- PTO does not roll over from year to year and is not paid out at separation.
- **Blackout dates:** the first two weekends of October (leaf season) are blackout dates — no PTO is approved for those weekends except for emergencies approved by the owner.
- Unpaid time off may be requested the same way and is approved at the Operations Manager's discretion.

## 4. Conduct and Uniform

- Uniform for all event staff: black Juniper Table polo, black pants, black non-slip shoes. Aprons are issued at the kitchen and returned after each event.
- Phones stay in the kitchen cubbies or the van during service. No exceptions while guests are present.
- We do not eat event food in view of guests. Staff meals are plated in the kitchen before service.
- Treat every guest's dietary request as important, even if it sounds unusual. Route all allergy questions through the Allergen Policy — never improvise an answer about ingredients.

## 5. Pay

- Payday is every other Friday by direct deposit.
- Your pay rate, position, and any tip-pool arrangement are set in your individual offer letter, which is the controlling document for compensation questions. This handbook does not list pay amounts for any position.
- Report any paycheck error to the Operations Manager within 7 days of payday.

## 6. Safety

- All staff follow the Food Safety & Allergen Policy and the Incident Report Procedure without exception.
- If you are injured at an event, tell the Event Captain immediately — even if it seems minor. See the Incident Report Procedure for the form and deadlines.
