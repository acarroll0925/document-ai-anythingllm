# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Delivery & Vehicle Policy

*Covers the company van and any personal vehicle approved for a delivery run.*

## 1. Who May Drive

- Drivers must be **21 or older**, hold a valid Georgia driver's license, and have a clean motor-vehicle report on file with the Operations Manager.
- Before driving solo, a new driver completes one supervised checkout ride with the Event Captain or Operations Manager.
- No one drives a company vehicle after drinking alcohol, full stop. The Captain reassigns the drive, no questions and no penalty for asking.

## 2. Service Radius and Mileage Fee

- Delivery and setup are **included for events within 40 road miles of the Foundry Lane kitchen**.
- **Beyond 40 miles, a mileage fee of $1.85 per mile applies to each mile over 40**, measured one-way from the kitchen to the venue. The fee is quoted on the client's invoice before the event — never added as a surprise afterward.
- Events beyond 90 miles one-way require the owner's approval before booking.

## 3. Pre-Trip Inspection

Before every departure, the driver completes the pre-trip checklist on the van clipboard:

- Fuel at or above half a tank
- Tire pressure and visible condition
- Refrigeration unit running and at or below 41°F before loading
- Load secured: hot boxes latched, coolers strapped, allergen bin (blue) loaded last and accessible first

The signed checklist travels with the van and is filed with the event paperwork on return.

## 4. Fuel and Receipts

- The van's fuel card is used only for the van. The PIN is kept with the Operations Manager, not in the vehicle.
- Every fuel receipt goes in the glove-box envelope the same day. Missing receipts are reported to the Operations Manager within 24 hours.
- Personal vehicles approved for a delivery run are reimbursed at the same per-mile basis as Section 2, logged on the event sheet.
