# Juniper Table Catering Co. — Food Safety & Allergen Policy

*Applies to every event, every staff member, no exceptions. If this policy and a client's request conflict, the policy wins and the Event Captain explains why.*

## 1. Temperature Rules

- Cold food is held at **41°F or below**. Hot food is held at **135°F or above**. The range between is the danger zone — food may not sit in it for more than 2 hours total, including transport.
- The Event Captain logs cold-holding and hot-holding temperatures at load-out, on arrival, and every 2 hours during service.
- Anything that has been in the danger zone past the limit is discarded, never re-served. When in doubt, throw it out and tell the Captain.

## 2. Handwashing and Gloves

- Wash hands on arrival, before handling food, after touching your face or hair, after handling raw protein, and **at least every 30 minutes during service**.
- Gloves are changed whenever hands are washed, and always between handling raw protein and handling ready-to-eat food.

## 3. Cross-Contact Basics

- Raw proteins are stored below and away from ready-to-eat food in every cooler, including in the van.
- The **allergen station** is the prep surface marked with the **blue cutting board**. Only allergen-free remakes are prepared there, with sanitized utensils that live in the blue bin.

## 4. Nut Allergy Procedure

When a guest tells any staff member about a **peanut or tree-nut allergy**, follow these steps in order — every time, even if the dish "should be fine":

1. **Stop.** Do not serve or hand over any dish. Thank the guest, and write the allergy on the order ticket in red marker.
2. **Tell the Event Captain immediately.** No one handles a nut-allergy plate alone.
3. **Remake at the allergen station.** The dish is remade from scratch at the blue-board allergen station, with fresh gloves and the sanitized utensils from the blue bin. Never re-plate or pick nuts off an existing dish.
4. **Flag the plate.** The finished plate gets a **red allergen pick** before it leaves the kitchen line.
5. **The Event Captain carries it — alone.** Only the Captain delivers an allergen plate, carried separately, never on a shared tray with other dishes.
6. **Log it.** The Captain records the allergy, the dish, and the guest's table in the event allergen log before service ends.

## 5. Other Allergies

For the other major allergens (milk, egg, soy, wheat, fish, shellfish, sesame), the same allergen-station remake, flag, and separate-carry rules apply. If a guest's allergy is not covered by this policy or you are unsure whether a dish is safe, the answer is always: stop and get the Event Captain. Never guess about ingredients — if it is not on the recipe card, we do not claim it.
